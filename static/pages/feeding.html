Feeding


Feed the hermit Krabz fresh fruit and veggies. Our Aussie Krabz also love meat such as fish and chicken, pumpkin seeds, dried shrimp and cuttle bone is good for them too. They will also eat the driftwood and coco fiber if you use it as substrate.

Food

Dry foods

Our mixed dry food is formulated with the perfect indgredients that will keep your hermit Krabz healthy and happy! Some of the main ingredients include;

  • Shrimp, fish, cuttlebone
  • Safe veggies and fruits
  • Seeds, flower petels.

Fish flakes are okay to feed them, but only as an addition to a mixed diet! Krabz must not be only fed dry foods!

Wet foods

Being omnivorous scavangers, hermies are very easy to feed! They can eat fresh veggies, fruits, meats and flowers. Avoid all sauces and seasonings as they can be toxic to them.

Unsafe foods

  • Tomato branches
  • onions
  • Table salt (iodine is extremely toxic!)
  • Citrus fruits like lemons and oranges
  • Onions
  • Garlic
  • Gum leaves

Water

Fresh water

They need fresh water pools to drink and bathe in. Having more than one fresh water pool will help keep the humidity levels up (which need to be up to 80%). Don't worry if the Krabz tip a little bit of the water onto the sand, it will keep the tank environment moist and humid.

Larger fresh water sources are a good idea as long as they have something to help them climb out. Be careful usinf driftwood for this, as it might make the wood rot or grow mould. Remove mouldy wood from the aquarium.

Saltwater

Hermies need access to salt water, especially when they are about to moult. You only need one salt water pool in their aquarium. Make sure it is cleaned and topped up regularly.

Some people believe you should "bathe" hermit crabs in salt water to keep them from fighting (when introducing new krabs) but here at Krabz, we don't believe it is needed. Feel free to if you want to, but we don't encourage it either because in some cases it can be a stressful experience for the krabz.
